# Sample single-SKU briefs

## Hot

Create a Cardamom Cloud Latte. Use the hot format. The drink should be warm beige with fine microfoam and light cardamom dust. Place a small natural cluster of green cardamom pods on the left. Keep the right and back zones empty. Add only subtle steam.

## Iced

Create a Mango Matcha Iced Latte. Use the iced format. Show a golden mango base, white milk centre, and green matcha top with clear ice and light condensation. Put ripe mango slices on the left and a small clear bowl of matcha powder on the right. Keep the back empty.

## Frappe

Create a Salted Caramel Frappe. Use the frappe format. The drink should be a thick pale caramel-brown blend with fresh irregular whipped cream and caramel drizzle. Place caramel cubes on the left and a small clear jug of cream on the right. Do not add a straw.

## Protein

Create a Cocoa Almond Protein Shake. Use the protein format. Show an opaque cocoa-brown drink with moderate natural thickness, full powder integration, restrained foam, and light cocoa dust. Place whole almonds on the left and a small clear bowl of cocoa powder on the right.

## Slush

Create a Watermelon Lime Slush. Use the slush format. Show a translucent coral-red drink with fine, densely packed ice crystals and slight natural colour variation. Place fresh watermelon wedges on the left and halved limes on the right. Do not add a straw or mint.
